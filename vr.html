Interactive VR Experience - Poetry Adaptation

This immersive VR experience is a virtual cinematic adaptation of two poems written by Kevin O'Farrel - Sight Reading and Untitled (Portrait of a Gentleman). It was created in for the client Poetry Ireland.

If you are interested in the process behind the production of this, check out the behind the scenes video here.

The Last Prophecy of Madame Ophelia - Interactive VR Experience

A still scene depicting the narrative of the last prophecy of Madame Ophelia. Created as part of narrative world-building project, this Unreal Engine Project condenses an entire story into a single "diorama". The goal of this piece was to design a single, detailed shot that feels like a pause in a larger story.

WIP - Game with the the themes of Irish Mythology

A narrative-driven game inspired by Irish mythology, currently in development by our little team of 3 people. The project reimagines old stories, creatures, and symbols in a more atmospheric, personal way rather than as pure fantasy combat.

These are a few teaser storyboards I created to introduce the world and atmosphere of the game.

This shoe was designed and sculpted entirely in Virtual Reality using the software Gravity Sketch and HTC Vive, to show that you can also create assets in Virtual Reality and not only for it.
