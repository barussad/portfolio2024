Too Good To Go - App Redesign

TGTG is built around a simple mission: reduce food waste by letting people rescue unsold food from nearby shops. The concept is strong, but the existing interface feels busy and unwelcoming. My goal was to create a cleaner, faster Too Good To Go experience designed around clarity, trust, and the joy of rescuing food.
